Request account and data deletion

In the game, open Settings → Delete account. Verify the same linked Apple or Google account. If both providers are linked, verify each one.

You can also request deletion by email without the app. Include your user ID if known. Writing from the email used to link the account helps us verify ownership. Support processes the request after verifying ownership; a user ID alone does not authorize deletion.

Write deletion request↗

info@anshin-labs.com

Progress, coins and materials, soldiers and heroes, village, profile drawings, authored chats, rankings and account links are deleted. You leave your tribe and leadership passes to a remaining member. This cannot be undone. Your Apple or Google account itself is not deleted. Store refunds are separate.

Anonymous purchase identifier hashes preventing duplicate rewards and anonymous deletion receipts are retained. Service provider backups expire under their retention policies.
