Data and purposes

We store account identifiers and email provided when you link an account; game progress, currencies and purchase identifiers; and nicknames, drawings and chats you submit. We use these for sign-in, save recovery, gameplay, reward verification, abuse prevention and support. The app does not store payment card details.

Visibility

World and tribe chats, nicknames, flag drawings, rankings and tribal war information may be visible to other users of those features. Suggestions are visible only to their author and operators.

Providers and ads

Supabase handles authentication and server storage. Apple and Google handle sign-in and store purchases. Google AdMob and UMP may process device and advertising identifiers, IP addresses, usage and diagnostic information for ads, consent, measurement and fraud prevention. Manage available choices through iOS tracking permission and ad privacy settings. Provider servers may be located in other countries.

Retention and choices

We keep records needed to provide the game while your account remains active. Account deletion removes the data described above, retaining anonymous purchase replay prevention hashes. Provider retention policies are linked below. Contact support with privacy questions or deletion requests.
