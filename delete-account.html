How to request deletion

To request deletion of your account and related data, please follow the steps below.

  1. Send an account deletion request email to the address below.
  2. Include the email address or user ID used for registration in the email body.
  3. After identity verification, your account and related data will be deleted.
Request account deletion↗

info@anshin-labs.com

Data that will be deleted

  • Account information
  • Email address
  • User ID
  • Game progress / saved data
  • Data related to app usage

Data that may be retained

Some data may be retained for a necessary period for legal compliance, fraud prevention, payment records, or security purposes. The retention period is up to 90 days.

Partial data deletion

If you want to delete only part of your data without deleting your entire account, please contact us at the same email address.

After account deletion, game data or some features may not be restored.
